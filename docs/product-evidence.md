## Wording limits

- Describe observable behavior: Lootlog does not move the character, choose
  combat actions, or play on the user's behalf.
- Do not promise that using Lootlog cannot result in sanctions.
- Identify Lootlog as an unofficial community project.
- Do not publish testimonials.
- Update adoption metrics with their definition, date range, and verification
  date. A lifetime account count is not MAU.
