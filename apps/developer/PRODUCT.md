# Developer-portal product context

Read the canonical [`PRODUCT.md`](../../PRODUCT.md) first.

The developer portal is not yet a supported product surface. It launches only
with at least one supported integration, a versioned API, scoped and revocable
credentials, usage limits, examples, and a compatibility policy. Until then,
do not promote the placeholder or imply that internal OpenAPI contracts are a
supported third-party API.
