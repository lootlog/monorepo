# Landing product context

Read the canonical [`PRODUCT.md`](../../PRODUCT.md) first. This file defines the
landing app's local responsibilities.

## Purpose

The landing app helps a leader or player understand Lootlog, evaluate its
limits, install the Game client, open the Web app, and find legal or community
resources.

## Responsibilities

- Explain the connected Game client and Web app as one product.
- Lead with live awareness, durable records, and Organization coordination.
- Move an existing Discord community toward bot installation and member setup.
- Publish current legal documents and links to docs, GitHub, Discord, and
  support.

## Claim rules

- Use real product screenshots and approved evidence from
  `docs/product-evidence.md`.
- Do not publish testimonials.
- Do not claim official Garmory approval or guaranteed safety from sanctions.
- State observable limits: Lootlog does not move the character, choose combat
  actions, or play for the user.
- Publish adoption counts only when a dated MAU definition and result are
  approved.

## Constraints

- The app is prerendered by TanStack Start and deploys only `dist/client`.
- It is an adoption surface, not the user documentation or authenticated app.
- Follow the Persuade mode in `DESIGN.md`.
- Polish is the supported product language and all copy remains behind i18n.
