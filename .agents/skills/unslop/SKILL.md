# Unslop

Edit the text, not its meaning.

## Process

1. Preserve facts, uncertainty, citations, terminology, formatting requirements, and the original language.
2. Delete filler, puffery, fake enthusiasm, vague attribution, and generic conclusions.
3. Replace abstract or promotional wording with concrete facts, mechanisms, paths, symbols, or measured results.
4. Use plain words, active voice, varied sentence length, and one main thought per sentence.
5. Keep a point of view when the author has one. Do not make all writing sterile or artificially casual.
6. Read the result once for obvious AI patterns and once for accidental loss of meaning.

## Common tells

- empty openings such as "Of course", "Great question", or "It is important to note";
- stock vocabulary such as "delve", "pivotal", "landscape", "tapestry", "showcase", or "testament";
- forced groups of three and repeated "not only X, but Y" constructions;
- excessive headings, bold labels, em dashes, colons, and decorative emoji;
- synonym cycling for one domain term;
- hedging without epistemic value;
- claims that could appear unchanged in any project's documentation.

Do not remove useful structure, necessary caution, conventional technical terms, or the directness expected in Polish and English engineering communication.
